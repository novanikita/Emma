Voice agent market research

Emma

A library of findings for a product that will complement the Diptalk ecosystem. What Emma shares with Diptalk is speech, and signals above the transcript. Emma is aimed at conversation, and at understanding one person: her interlocutor. The point is the quality of conversation as a process; solving tasks follows from that.

Principles

Starting hypotheses. We will refine this list as the research goes on.

  1. Small startups can offer higher-quality voice agents than OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and other large models.
  2. Emma’s value is in empathy.
  3. Emma’s value is in focusing on the process, not the outcome.
